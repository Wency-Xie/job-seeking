# CompanySheet research and mapping

Use this map on every company run, including reworking a legacy report. Research is company-specific; the existing CompanySheet fields are the output format. Do not copy another company's content to fill cells.

| Section ID | Questions and source strategy | Output and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `industry-position` | Define the actual subsector and value-chain position. What inputs, know-how, equipment, data or channels enable delivery? Who uses, procures, pays, and benefits? Read product workflows, filings' business-model sections, regulator or industry-body material. | `arena`, `industryTrack`, `valueChain`, `fact`; `industryContext.upstream/downstream` and `industryContext.analysis` must be populated for new research. Define primary/adjacent industry, evidence-based company standing, current conditions and conditional future directions with observable signals alongside the preserved value chain. Keep the full value chain first; show actual customer/supplier records from `profile.organization.revenueChain` next, then industry position/current/outlook as separate reading tabs with expandable evidence. Storage under organization does not make counterparties a financial-panel section. Explain inputs → company activity → deliverables → customer value. Category-level analysis is permitted and must be marked inference, never presented as actual supplier/customer relationships. |

Group by actual business line, not every SKU. | `products`, `scenarios`, `users`, `businessLines` all populated. Every business line has `name`, `offer`, `user`, `buyer`, `workflow`, `roleValue`. Explain what is delivered, to whom, who pays, delivery stages and the supplied role's contribution. With no JD, roleValue stays empty. |
| `competition-landscape` | Research competitors' own product/service pages, not just the target's claims. Compare the same use case and purchasing budget. If JD absent, mark coverage not_applicable with reason; do not guess. |

## Source and extraction discipline

1. Establish entity and product boundaries. Reuse valid old facts by meaning. Preserve original report; generate a new pending revision.
2. Search per uncovered section. Start with target official sources/filings, then regulator, trade body, and competitor primary sources appropriate to the claim. Search snippets are leads; open the underlying source before citing it. Record attempts including failures.
3. Track claim dates separately from access dates. For conflicting figures, compare period, entity and metric before choosing; retain unresolved conflicts as questions. A report published recently may describe an older period.
4. Map each useful claim into its destination, retaining fact indexes. A paragraph may support multiple fields, but one generic financial fact cannot substantiate all sections. Industry inference must name its reasoning and evidence limit.
5. Check coverage and actual content together. `covered` means the fields answer the section questions, not merely that a URL was found. `public_gap` needs attempted-source indexes and the precise unavailable information. `unresearched` and `mapping_error` force incomplete output until resolved.
6. Validate JSON against output.schema.json and the application's validator before delivery. If validation exposes a mapping or shape error, repair it; never convert it to a public-information gap. Keep the last valid draft when search or validation fails.

## Final read-through

Read the result as the fixed page: can the user follow industry input, actual company work, products, users/payers, each business line, alternatives, operating signals and role implications? All displayed claims need annotations and working source links. Summaries and financial tables cannot substitute for missing sections. The saved result remains pending; never accept it for the user.

## Organization depth acceptance

Apply the v5 Organization and operating research depth section of SKILL.md. Map the four researchDepth checks to real source evidence, not a blanket covered flag. The display consumes timeline context/sourceUrl, leadership date/speaker/sourceUrl, financials.metrics and revenueChain. Preserve the exact legal entity boundary in every group metric and counterpart record. History starts at the founding/legal origin and connects at least three substantive sourced nodes to relevant policy/market triggers and transformation when claiming covered. Leadership seeks multiple speeches/interviews in the trailing 3–5 years, with at least three dated sources across three calendar years for covered; compare signals and later actions without inferring personality. Finance seeks 3–5 comparable complete years, with fewer than three accessible years marked public_gap, and keeps the latest interim separate. Revenue/procurement traces concrete offerings and counterparties without turning cooperation into income or purchases into sales. Missing detail remains a documented public_gap after targeted alternative searches; never fill a quota with weak or invented content. The precise requirements and follow-up stopping rule remain in SKILL.md.

Industry acceptance follows v6 in SKILL.md: source and annotate scope, position, each currentState/outlook item; separate observed conditions from forecasts and segment-specific rank from company self-description.


## Display mapping and review

| Content | Default view | Supporting detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Value chain and counterparties | Full chain first; customer/supplier identity, offering/theme, material amount and period nearby. Keep customer and procurement roles distinct. | Relationship evidence, exact undisclosed granularity and source via existing revenueChain fields. Do not infer named counterparties from category nodes. |
| Industry analysis | Primary segment and supported company position; concise current mechanisms and conditional directions. | Full observation, impact, conditions, watch indicators and source links through existing analysis fields. Preserve the chain when adding this analysis. |
| Evolution and leadership | Origin and meaningful transformation nodes; dated leadership themes and concise observed signals. | Context, supporting materials, implications and source links. Preserve speaker/title and fact/inference boundaries. |
| Financials | Comparable annual trends; latest interim and reconciled composition kept distinct, alongside each other when space/data permit. | Expandable exact annual values, R&D definitions and operating explanations; charts and detailed values must agree. |

Keep decisive limitations next to the claim, even when other details are folded: group versus subsidiary, annual versus interim, disclosed aggregate versus named-client amount, cooperation versus revenue, purchases versus income. Use existing visible fields for a concise qualification when necessary; annotations alone are insufficient for these limits. Avoid repeating full evidence/limitation paragraphs at equal prominence.

Review depth and reading separately. Research quotas remain unchanged; concise presentation must not reduce source work. Layout is adaptive, not a fixed company template: no empty symmetry panels, unnecessary repeated figures or charts without appropriate data. Preserve full details and sources through the existing components. Read SKILL.md's Reading hierarchy and presentation acceptance before the final desktop/mobile review. Do not modify the renderer as part of a research run; disclose a renderer limitation if it prevents the requested presentation.
